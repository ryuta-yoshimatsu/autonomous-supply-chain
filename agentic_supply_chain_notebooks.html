02 Generate demand forecast

Builds a forward demand forecast per material × plant × channel from sales history (statsforecast AutoETS with a seasonal-naive fallback), scores it with a rolling-origin backtest, and emits conformal calibration error-sets for the inventory policy. The forecast is written to monitoring.demandplan so the source schema stays read-only (forecast_output_table overrides it).

Inputsgold.salesorders
Outputs demandplan demand_forecast_accuracy demand_forecast_error_sets

04 Demand twin

Materializes the Demand / Customer twin: firm & open demandiFirm demand — actual booked customer sales orders (what customers have already ordered), as opposed to the statistical forecast.

Open demand — firm orders not yet delivered whose requested delivery date is still ahead: the forward order book awaiting fulfilment., backlogiBacklog — firm orders now past their requested delivery date but still undelivered — overdue unfulfilled demand (ordered − delivered, past due). Driven by order type: only shipped orders (ZOR wholesale) are netted against deliveries; counter (ZPOS) and delivery (ZDEL) orders are fulfilled without a tracked delivery, so their open demand and backlog are 0. (Any residual backlog reflects genuinely-shipped lines whose delivery records are missing in the synthetic source.), forecast stateiForecast state — the current demand forecast the twin carries (from nb 02 / monitoring.demandplan): the forward predicted demand per period, with its prediction interval, used to project future requirements downstream., actual-vs-forecast accuracy/bias, demand volatility, and customer/product context. Everything downstream reads demand from here.

Inputs gold.salesordersdemandplan gold.outbounddeliveriesgold.materialsmd gold.customersmddemand_forecast_accuracy
Outputs demand_twin_currentdemand_twin_metrics_timeseries

05 Inventory twin

Inventory / material-flow twin: on-handiOn-hand — the current physical stock available now (unrestricted-use quantity) at the material × plant, from the latest inventory snapshot., consumption velocityiConsumption velocity — the rate stock is being drawn down: the trailing daily consumption rate (units/day). It converts a stock level into a time buffer (days of cover)., days-of-coveriDays of cover — how long current on-hand will last: on-hand ÷ daily rate. Reported as actual (trailing consumption) and demand (forecast). The risk flags use an effective cover = demand where a forecast exists, else trailing consumption — so dependent-only items (packaging, dairy) are still monitored, not just forecasted goods. Low = stockout risk; very high = overstock., stockout / low-cover / overstock flagsiRisk flags (thresholds from planning_config). Stockout: on-hand ≤ 0 — out of stock now. Low cover / Overstock: the effective days-of-cover (forecast demand where available, else trailing consumption) below the low-cover threshold (e.g. 14d) or above the overstock threshold (e.g. 90d) — so components without a forecast are still flagged. Items with neither a forecast nor a consumption rate can't be cover-flagged (handled by MRP)., and projected balancesiProjected balances — the forward stock trajectory given demand: e.g. the projected stockout date (when on-hand is expected to hit zero) and horizon coverage, so risk is visible before it happens, not only current state.. The connective tissue that links demand to supply.

Inputs gold.inventorysnapshotsdemand_twin_current planning_config
Outputs inventory_twin_currentinventory_twin_metrics_timeseries

06 Procurement twin

Inbound supply twin: open/past-due POsiOpen POs — purchase-order quantity not yet received (open_po_qty = ordered − received): inbound supply still on the way. Past-due — the share of open PO items whose expected delivery date has already passed (past_due_rate): overdue inbound., expected receiptsiExpected receipts — quantity still to arrive on open POs (expected_open_receipt_qty) plus the next expected receipt date (next_expected_receipt_date): the forward inbound-supply pipeline., supplier OTIF / on-time / in-fulliSupplier reliability rates (0–1, computed line-level). On-time — delivered by the requested date. In-full — the full ordered quantity delivered. OTIF — on-time and in-full: the strict combined measure (a line counts only if it met both)., lead time & variabilityiLead time — the average supplier order-to-receipt cycle time in days (avg_lead_time_days). Variability — its standard deviation (lead_time_std_days); higher = less predictable supply. These drive the replenishment lead time and safety stock downstream., rejectioniRejection — quantity rejected on receipt for quality, as a share of ordered qty (rejection_rate = rejected ÷ PO qty): a supplier quality signal., invoice accuracyiInvoice accuracy — share of supplier invoices that match the PO / receipt (invoice_accuracy_rate): billing / financial reliability., and supplier-risk flagiSupplier-risk flag — a boolean set TRUE when OTIF is below the risk threshold or rejection is above it (thresholds from planning_config, e.g. OTIF < 0.80 or rejection > 0.05). Marks suppliers to watch or act on..

Inputs gold.vendorperformanceplanning_config
Outputs procurement_twin_currentprocurement_twin_metrics_timeseries

07 Production twin

Manufacturing twin: open production orders / WIPiOpen production orders / WIP — orders not yet complete (open_order_count) and the work-in-progress quantity still to be produced (open_production_qty): what's currently on the shop floor, unfinished., planned-vs-actualiPlanned-vs-actual — planned output (planned_qty) versus what was actually produced (produced_qty), plus cost variance (avg_cost_variance_pct): the production attainment / execution gap., yield & scrapiYield & scrap — yield_pct = good output as a share of input; scrap_pct = share wasted; first_pass_yield_pct = right-first-time. Process-quality signals. (Item-level unweighted averages; a quantity-weighted variant is an extension.), schedule adherenceiSchedule adherence — share of orders that met their planned schedule (schedule_adherence_rate), with on_time_start_rate for on-time starts: how closely execution follows the plan., recorded lead timeiRecorded lead time — the actual observed order start-to-finish time (avg_actual_lead_time_hours) and its variability (avg_lead_time_variance_hours). These are recorded actuals from the order header — not a capacity / scheduling model., primary work centreiPrimary work centre — the work centre the material's production routes through (e.g. ROASTER, PACKING), now taken from the routings master (routed_work_centre), falling back to the order header when no routing exists., routed work content & work backlogiRouted work content & work backlog — routed_work_minutes_per_unit (standard minutes per unit from the routing) × open quantity = open_work_content_minutes. Divided by the work centre's work_centre_daily_capacity_min this gives work_backlog_days = days to clear open work at full capacity. Magnitude is inflated by synthetic open quantities, but relative values identify the capacity pressure point (the roaster)., and bottleneck flagiBottleneck flag — is_bottleneck = the OR of workcentres.IsBottleneckFlag across the material's work centres (a master-data designation). Kept as the reliable signal; the routing-based work_backlog_days above is exposed alongside it as an informational capacity-pressure metric..

Inputs gold.productionordersgold.productionordersheader gold.workcentresgold.routings
Outputs production_twin_currentproduction_twin_metrics_timeseries

08 Distribution twin

Outbound fulfillment twin: deliveriesiDeliveries — outbound delivery lines in the window (delivery_line_count) with ordered vs delivered quantity (ordered_qty / delivered_qty) and the open shipment book (open_delivery_qty = ordered − delivered on lines not yet goods-issued)., fill rateiFill rate — delivered ÷ ordered quantity (fill_rate): the quantity-weighted share of demand actually fulfilled., on-time / in-full / OTIFiService-reliability rates (line-level). On-time — delivered by the due date. In-full — the full ordered quantity delivered. OTIF — on-time and in-full: the strict combined measure (a NULL on-time counts as not on-time, so OTIF reads slightly stricter than on-time alone)., transit transitiPlanned vs actual transit — avg_planned_transit_days from transport requirements against avg_actual_transit_days derived from the assigned shipments (real ship-to-arrive time), replacing the old derived estimate., window feasibility & service latenessiWindow feasibility & service lateness — window_feasible_rate = share of delivery lines whose requested ship window is achievable; avg_service_lateness_days = average lateness vs that window. From transportrequirements (1:1 with delivery lines)., freight costiFreight cost — freight_cost = total delivery freight cost over the window (from transport requirements): the cost side of fulfilment, now available per material × plant × channel., fulfillment-risk flagiFulfillment-risk flag — a boolean set TRUE when otif_rate is below the risk threshold (from planning_config). Marks series at risk of missing delivery commitments., and transport-risk flagiTransport-risk flag — transport_risk_flag = TRUE when any requested ship window is infeasible (window_feasible_rate < 1): flags lanes/series where the promised delivery window can't be met by transport..

Planning engines

14 Inventory policy

Inventory optimization for independent-demand itemsiIndependent-demand items — items whose demand comes directly from customers (sales orders / forecast), not from producing something else — e.g. finished goods sold as-is and purchased goods sold over the counter. Their demand is forecast statistically, so they get a reorder point / order-up-to target here. Contrast dependent demand, which is derived from a parent item's production via the BOM (e.g. packaging, raw beans) — those are sized by MRP (nb 15), not here.: a distribution-free split-conformal reorder point / order-up-to (S* = μ + safety), scoped by demand independence — purchased sold items → PO, produced sold items → PRODUCTION target, dependent-only items flagged for MRP. Writes recommendations onto open inventory events.

Inputs inventory_twin_currentprocurement_twin_current production_twin_currentdemand_twin_metrics_timeseries demand_forecast_error_setsplanning_config
Outputs inventory_policyevents (recommended_action)

15 MRP / supply plan

Time-phased weekly MRP over a 13-week horizon: consumes nb 14's order-up-to policy and applies it week by week (week 1 = nb 14's decision), then explodes the resulting planned production through the BOM into dependent, lead-time-offset planned orders (PURCHASE / PRODUCTION).

Inputs v_material_bom_edgesdemand_twin_metrics_timeseries inventory_twin_currentprocurement_twin_current production_twin_currentinventory_policy planning_config
Outputs mrp_plan (weekly grain)events (recommended_action)

All monitoring.* tables/views are rebuilt deterministically (idempotent overwrite) each run. Pipeline order matters where noted: 02 → 03 (forecast before demand twin), 03 → 04 (demand twin feeds inventory days-of-cover), twins → 08/09/12, and 12 → 14 → 15 (events are detected, then the planning engines fill their recommendations; re-running 12 resets them).
