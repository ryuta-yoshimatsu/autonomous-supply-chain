3Data Readiness — Gaps Filled vs Still Missing

Moving to mickmickstableaws_catalog.gold closed most of the earlier gaps: inventory, outbound deliveries, and billing are now present. The demandplan table exists but is empty, so generating the demand forecast is the first prerequisite. What remains to build is the demand forecast, transport layer, distribution network model, planning parameters, production routings & scheduler, and the plan-of-record store. Cards below are marked Now in gold, Empty / first step, or Missing.

0–1 · Demand Demand forecast Empty / first step

The demandplan table exists but has 0 rows. Before hydrating the Demand Twin, generate a Databricks-native demand forecast from sales history and write forecast rows to the agreed demand-plan table.

Blocks

Demand Twin forecast state, forecast accuracy / bias, inventory projections, MRP netting, production priorities, and distribution planning.

How we fill it

Run the demand forecasting job first, then hydrate demand_twin_current and demand_twin_timeseries.

1 · Demand Order → requested-delivery lead time Data realism gap

In gold.salesorders, RequestedDeliveryDate_VDATU averages 0 days after CreationDate_ERDAT — every order is requested for same-day delivery. With no order-to-request lead time, the forward order book (open demand) collapses to ~1 week and overdue demand is overstated.

Affects

Demand Twin open-demand vs backlog split, and any due-date / promise-date planning.

How we fill it

Fix in synthetic data generation (out of scope for this build): set RequestedDeliveryDate_VDATU = CreationDate_ERDAT + a realistic, channel/material-specific lead time.

2 · Inventory On-hand inventory Now in gold

Stock-on-hand and movements are now available: inventorysnapshots, inventoryledger, and inventoryopeningstock in gold — point-in-time stock can be reconstructed.

Unblocks

Days-of-cover, stockout risk, safety-stock targets, inventory optimization, MRP netting.

Source

gold.inventorysnapshots / inventoryledger / inventoryopeningstock

2–3 · Inventory / Supply Planning parameters master Missing

No service levels, safety-stock policies, reorder points, or a consolidated lead-time master — the "rules" the inventory and MRP engines optimize against.

Blocks

Inventory optimization, MRP policy, S&OP targets, scenario constraints.

How we fill it

Model a planning_parameters master (per material: service level, lead time, policy).

4 · Production Production routings & finite scheduler Missing

No routing master (operation sequence, standard times per work centre) and no sequence-dependent setup / changeover matrix; calendars are day-level only. Routings are merely referenced on order headers and observed after the fact in productionconfirmations.

Blocks

Finite-capacity scheduling, order sequencing, realistic start/finish times, bottleneck planning.

How we fill it

Model a routings master + setup matrix, then build the finite-capacity scheduler (heuristic + OR-Tools).

5 · Distribution Outlet / distribution network Missing

The data describes a central roastery only — the multi-echelon nodes (outlets / DCs) and their demand are not modelled.

Blocks

Multi-echelon inventory, distribution planning, outlet replenishment, network optimization.

How we fill it

Synthesize an outlet master + outlet-level demand allocation from sales history.

5 · Distribution Outbound deliveries Now in gold

Outbound fulfillment is now modelled in outbounddeliveries (actual ship date, delivered qty, linked to sales orders), with billinginvoices for the cash side.

Unblocks

Outbound OTIF, fill rate, perfect-order, delivery plan-vs-actual, order-to-cash.

Source

gold.outbounddeliveries + gold.billinginvoices

5 · Distribution Delivery coverage of demand Data realism gap

gold.outbounddeliveries holds shipments for only 3 of the 21 sold materials (2,148 lines). The other 18 materials have no DeliveryQuantity_LFIMG / ReferenceSDDocument_VGBEL rows, so when the Demand Twin nets the order book against deliveries (ordered − delivered) those materials appear 100% unfulfilled.

Affects

Demand Twin backlog (ordered − delivered), Distribution Twin fill rate / OTIF coverage.

How we fill it

Fix in synthetic data generation (out of scope for this build): generate outbounddeliveries for all shipped materials, linked via ReferenceSDDocument_VGBEL / ReferenceSDDocumentItem_VGPOS.

5 · Distribution Shipments & transport Missing

Route_ROUTE is empty and there is a single shipping point across all 1.9M order lines — no carrier, route, or freight data.

Blocks

Freight cost, route / lane performance, transport optimization, distribution planning.

How we fill it

Synthesize shipments (carrier, route, freight cost, ship / arrive timestamps).

4Design Strengtheners — Make It Robust & Realistic

These guardrails keep the system agentic and operationally useful without pretending it is fully autonomous from day one.

Human approval for consequential actions

Agents can diagnose, simulate, draft, and recommend. Planner approval is required before committing a new plan, changing production schedules, expediting purchase orders, or contacting customers / suppliers.

Evidence-backed recommendations

Every recommendation should cite the KPI deviation, affected materials / orders / customers, likely root cause, scenario comparison, and expected service / cost impact before asking for approval.

Separate source facts from planning state

Keep gold as read-only source-of-truth. Write forecasts, policies, scenarios, recommendations, approvals, and committed plans to a separate planning store (Lakebase + Delta).

Bounded agent tools and audit logs

Expose narrow governed tools (run scenario, draft supplier note, propose schedule change, notify owner). Log every tool call, input, output, approver, and committed action for traceability.

Fail-safe automation

If data is stale, policies are missing, or a solver fails, the agent should stop at diagnosis and escalation — not invent a plan or take action on uncertain inputs.

Materialized KPI and plan layers

Precompute KPI time series and plan outputs incrementally. Dashboards, alerts, and agents should read stable curated tables, not recompute heavy logic from base facts on every interaction.

Expectation to set: we can build a credible Databricks-native functional surface for agentic supply chain planning — demand, supply, inventory, production scheduling, distribution, S&OP, scenario / what-if, control tower, and agent-assisted orchestration. We will not match the depth of fully-concurrent, multi-echelon optimization that specialist planning suites (e.g. OMP) have tuned over decades; we approximate it with Databricks-native forecasting + targeted solver-based modules + the orchestration agent, planned heuristic-first with a re-plan loop. For a POC of "orchestrate the entire supply chain," that is more than enough to be compelling and extensible.
