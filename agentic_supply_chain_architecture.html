Agentic Supply Chain Orchestration

A closed-loop planning platform where agents help planners watch, reason, recommend, and act with approval — sensing demand, generating supply / production / inventory / distribution plans, optimizing across constraints, simulating scenarios, and re-planning when reality drifts. 4Design Strengtheners — Make It Robust & Realistic

These guardrails keep the system agentic and operationally useful without pretending it is fully autonomous from day one.

Human approval for consequential actions

Agents can diagnose, simulate, draft, and recommend. Planner approval is required before committing a new plan, changing production schedules, expediting purchase orders, or contacting customers / suppliers.

Evidence-backed recommendations

Every recommendation should cite the KPI deviation, affected materials / orders / customers, likely root cause, scenario comparison, and expected service / cost impact before asking for approval.

Separate source facts from planning state

Keep gold_opus5 as read-only source-of-truth. Write forecasts, policies, scenarios, recommendations, approvals, and committed plans to a separate planning store (Lakebase + Delta).

Bounded agent tools and audit logs

Expose narrow governed tools (run scenario, draft supplier note, propose schedule change, notify owner). Log every tool call, input, output, approver, and committed action for traceability.

Fail-safe automation

If data is stale, policies are missing, or a solver fails, the agent should stop at diagnosis and escalation — not invent a plan or take action on uncertain inputs.

Materialized KPI and plan layers

Precompute KPI time series and plan outputs incrementally. Dashboards, alerts, and agents should read stable curated tables, not recompute heavy logic from base facts on every interaction.

Expectation to set: we can build a credible Databricks-native functional surface for agentic supply chain planning — demand, supply, inventory, production scheduling, distribution, S&OP, scenario / what-if, control tower, and agent-assisted orchestration. We will not match the depth of fully-concurrent, multi-echelon optimization that specialist planning suites (e.g. OMP) have tuned over decades; we approximate it with Databricks-native forecasting + targeted solver-based modules + the orchestration agent, planned heuristic-first with a re-plan loop. For a POC of "orchestrate the entire supply chain," that is more than enough to be compelling and extensible.
